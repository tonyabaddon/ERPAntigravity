## 1. Purpose

Execution roadmap untuk 6 bulan pertama VOSI go-to-market. Bukan strategy slide — execution document. Setiap bulan punya goal terukur, KPI yang ditrack, trigger condition ke bulan berikutnya.

Bulan M1-M6 = relatif ke **tenant pertama signed**, bukan kalender absolut. Kalau tenant #1 signed pertengahan Juli 2026 → M1 = pertengahan Juli – pertengahan Agustus.

## 2. Strategy summary (locked)

| Aspek | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| **Target segment (primary)** | Distributor B2B Indonesia — sentra Glodok (alat listrik, panel, CCTV, suku cadang) |
| **Default tier pitch** | **Pro 12-month Rp 664K/bln** (Pro 6-month Rp 807K/bln sebagai fallback) |
| **Premium positioning** | **Warm upsell only** dari Pro base 3-6 bulan aktif. Tidak cold acquisition Premium di M1-M2. |
| **Starter posture** | **Accept inbound, tidak push.** Cap Starter mix ≤25% (lebih ketat dari pricing.md guardrail 35%). |
| **Pricing** | v3 — 6mo (15% off) + 12mo (30% off), no Quarterly. Setup fee Rp 1.5M Pro / Rp 3.5M Premium. 14-day money-back. |
| **Second vertical** | M5: chat-heavy online seller (Premium-led) — different campaign, different sub-page, different funnel. |
| **Rollout cadence** | Direct-launch to all eligible tenants once shipped (per [[direct-launch-skip-phased]] memory). No soft-launch / phased / canary. |

**Bigger picture:** Goal bukan profitability di M6 — itu Stage 6 (500+ tenants, year 3-4). **M1-M6 = acquisition + validation + onboarding efficiency.** KPI matter: tenant count, MRR growth %, churn signal, onboarding hours trend, Pro→Premium conversion rate.

## 5. Cumulative cash modeling — full 6-month picture (v3 pricing)

### Scenario summary

| Scenario | Tenants by M6 | MRR by M6 | Cumulative cash in | Cumulative cash out | **Net 6-month cash** |
|----------|----------------|------------|---------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Konservatif** (CS hire M4, no junior eng M6) | 25 | Rp 25jt | Rp 195jt | Rp 50jt | **+Rp 145jt** |
| **Realistic** (CS hire M4, junior eng hire M6) | 30 | Rp 40jt | Rp 240jt | Rp 75jt | **+Rp 165jt** |
| **Stretch** (vertical 2 outperforms, Premium share 25%+) | 35 | Rp 55jt | Rp 290jt | Rp 80jt | **+Rp 210jt** |

### Comparison vs v2 modeling

v2 (Quarterly + Yearly) modeling earlier produced +Rp 80-115jt 6-month net. **v3 produces +Rp 145-210jt — ~50-80% improvement** karena:
1. Per-tenant cash upfront +55% (6mo upfront vs Quarterly upfront)
2. List price +10% across all tiers (anchor inflation)
3. Higher commitment filter → less churn-driven cash recapture loss

### Konteks penting

- **Cash positive ≠ profit positive.** Per pricing.md, blended net margin negative sampai ~400-500 tenants (Stage 5-6). 6-month cash positive yang ditampilkan di sini = **setup fees + upfront subscription as runway**, NOT recurring profit.
- **Setup fee adalah sacred runway** (pricing.md Rule 3). Jangan dimasukkan ke recurring profitability calculation.
- **Founder salary sweat-equity** (pricing.md Rule 2 Stage 0-1) — kalau dihitung di OPEX dengan Rp 5-10jt founder cash salary, net 6mo cash drops ~Rp 30-60jt.

## 8. Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| **CAC creeps above LTV/CAC=3 threshold** | High M3+ | Margin collapse di Starter tier | Track weekly. Kill paid channels yang CAC > Rp 1.5jt. Pricing.md Rule 5 binding. |
| **Calista shadow auto-reply transition fails** | Medium | Premium acquisition halt; founder bottleneck di tuning manual | Cap Premium acquisition 1/bulan sampai full-auto stable. Use shadow as feature, not caveat. |
| **First tenant churn (M4-M6)** | Medium | Reputation damage (Glodok community kecil). WoM negative | Root-cause analysis sebelum lanjut acquisition. Refund 14-day money-back jika masih dalam window. |
| **Founder workload >60h/minggu sustained** | High | Burnout = product halt risk | Hire trigger ditarik forward 1 month. CS hire bisa antisipasi sebelum MRR Rp 15jt. |
| **Sales Order Penawaran bug masuk production** | Medium | M1 cohort churn | Soft validation di Garindo (legacy tenant) lewat shadow-test sebelum push ke M2 acquisition wave |
| **Competitor (Mekari) drops price match** | Low M1-M3 | Pricing power erodes | Differentiation cuma Calista AI + distributor verticalization. Tahan harga, lean ke positioning. |
| **Vertical 2 fails to convert** | Medium M5-M6 | Premium share stuck di 15% (di bawah target 20%) | Pivot vertical 2 to F&B distributor (chat-heavy juga). Atau pause, scale Glodok. |
| **Money-back guarantee abuse** | Low | Setup fee revenue leak | Frame "active <30 days" tightly. Day-of-onboarding ≠ active. Audit refund cases monthly. |
